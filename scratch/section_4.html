PhD in Agriculture

A PhD in Agriculture at Geeta University is an intensive research doctoral course. It provides superior education in study fields in agricultural science like agronomy, soil science, plant breeding, agricultural economics, entomology, and horticulture. The program concentrates on innovation, sustainable development, biotechnology, climate-smart agriculture, and policy formulation. 

The program is also intensive research, writing a thesis, and teaching, in some cases. The technology promotes creativity and analytical research work to solve problems in the field of agriculture and to enhance agricultural productivity. Therefore, it may be suitable in careers such as academic research and development, government policy-making,and agribusiness leadership.

Thrust Research Areas

On the basis of existing Phd in Agriculture academic activities, research output, infrastructure, and collaborations, the major thrust research areas of the School of Agricultural Sciences are:

  1. Sustainable & Regenerative Farming Systems: Natural farming, soil health management, organic nutrient sources, regenerative agriculture, and climate-resilient farming practices.
  2. Crop Production, Agronomy & Precision Agriculture: Integrated crop management, precision agriculture, protected cultivation, smart farming tools, and data-driven decision-making in agriculture.
  3. Horticulture, Medicinal & Aromatic Plants: Nursery management, protected cultivation, vegetable production, mushroom cultivation, and innovation in horticultural systems.
  4. Soil Science & Nutrient Management: Organic and inorganic nutrient delivery systems, vermicomposting, soil fertility improvement, and sustainable input management.
  5. Remote Sensing, GIS & Digital Agriculture: Application of Remote Sensing and GIS in agriculture through national-level e-training and capacity building (IIRS–ISRO).
  6. Agri-Entrepreneurship & Extension Education: Farm-based organisations, microgreens, mushroom production, village adoption, farmer awareness events, and technology dissemination.
National & International Collaborations

The School of Agricultural Sciences has established strong academic and research collaborations through formal MoUs and institutional linkages, including:

  • Indian Institute of Remote Sensing (IIRS), ISRO, Dehradun: Designated as a Nodal Centre for e-Training in Remote Sensing & GIS, strengthening geospatial applications in agriculture.
  • ICAR–National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (NBPGR), New Delhi: MoU for joint research, germplasm access, academic exchange, and capacity building.
  • Red Diamond Nursery, Dharamgarh (Panipat, Haryana): Industry–academia partnership for experiential learning, nursery practices, and commercial plant production.
  • PLANTICA–IARD, Dehradun (Uttarakhand): Collaboration in horticultural research, innovation, training, and technology exchange.

These collaborations support joint research, student training, FDPs, innovation activities, and extension programs.
